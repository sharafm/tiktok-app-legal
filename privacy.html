Privacy Policy

TikTok · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data TikTok (“the App”) accesses through TikTok and how it is used. The App is a personal tool used only to manage the operator’s own TikTok account.

1. Data we access

With your permission, granted through TikTok’s login screen, the App may access:

  • Basic profile: display name, username, avatar, open ID and profile link.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Your public videos: titles, descriptions, cover images, links and view/like/comment/share counts.
  • Publishing: the ability to upload and post videos or photos you choose to your own account.

The App does not access your messages, contacts, password or other users’ private data.

2. How we use it

Only to show you your own account and video performance and to publish content you have reviewed and approved. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Service providers

The App connects to TikTok through an integration platform (Composio), which stores the access token TikTok issues so the App can act on your behalf. It processes data only to provide this connection. No other third parties receive your TikTok data.

4. Storage and retention

We do not keep a separate database of your TikTok data. Data is fetched from TikTok when needed. Access tokens are kept only while the connection is active and are deleted when you disconnect.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Ask us to delete any data related to your account by emailing the address below. We respond within 30 days.

6. Security

Access uses TikTok’s official OAuth authorization over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We never see or store your TikTok password.

7. Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
